FoodWise

Eat Well. Stay You.

An AI-powered nutrition education assistant. It suggests practical, culturally familiar meals for each person’s real situation — budget, time, hunger, allergies, traditions — and explains why they work, so people gradually learn to make balanced choices on their own.

The problem

Most nutrition apps are calorie trackers or rigid meal plans. They rarely fit local food cultures, ignore everyday constraints like a 15-minute lunch break or a tight budget, and frame traditional dishes as “unhealthy”. Users follow instructions without understanding them — and drop out.

In Uzbekistan, daily meals revolve around beloved dishes like plov, shurpa and non — people need guidance that works with these foods, not against them.

Our solution — three pillars

1Context-Aware AI. Combines stable context (allergies, diet, culture, goals) with today’s situation (skipped meals, fatigue, budget in UZS, time, place) to suggest 2–3 realistic meals.

2Evidence-based education. Every suggestion has a “Why this works” explanation linked to WHO healthy-diet principles and a visual plate composition.

3Progressive Independence. Short, judgement-free exercises teach users to reason about meals themselves, reducing reliance on the app over time.

Five Principles. One Smarter Choice.

Four principles from WHO/FAO healthy-diet guidance + Satisfaction, FoodWise’s own proposed addition (not an official WHO principle).

WHOAdequacyEnough of what the body needs, without excess.
WHOBalanceA sensible mix of energy and macronutrients.
WHOModerationLimiting excess sugar, salt and some fats.
WHODiversityVariety across food groups.
FOODWISESatisfactionTaste, tradition and practicality — so habits last.

Health impact & scientific rigour

  • Promotes balanced, varied everyday eating while keeping cultural foods — plov is adjusted, not banned.
  • Confirmed allergies and dietary exclusions are enforced in every suggestion, follow-up and swap.
  • No diagnosis or guaranteed outcomes; medical conditions, emergencies and extreme dieting are redirected to professionals.
  • Real sources (WHO, FAO, Harvard), labelled apart from clinical (IFM) and coaching (IIN) perspectives.
  • Privacy by design: all personal and health data stays on the user’s device.

Technical implementation

  • React + TypeScript + Vite, mobile-first (375–430 px), deployed on GitHub Pages.
  • Interactive Demo engine: transparent rule-based recommender over 18 structured meals; handles budget, time, diet, dislikes, swaps and requested dishes.
  • Structured responses ready for a server-side LLM; the allergy filter stays as a final safety layer.
  • Dynamic SVG plate & bowl illustrations: share of plate area (not calories), inspired by the Harvard Healthy Eating Plate.
